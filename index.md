Kepta is a notification archive that runs entirely on your phone. This policy
explains exactly what it stores, where it stays, and what it never does.

---

## The short version

Kepta stores your notifications **only on your own device**. Nothing is uploaded,
nothing is shared, and there is no account, server, or analytics of any kind. The
developer cannot see your data, because it never leaves your phone.

---

## What Kepta reads

To do its job, Kepta uses Android's **Notification Access** permission. Android
delivers every notification your phone shows to any app holding that permission,
so Kepta technically receives all of them.

It only **saves** notifications from apps you have explicitly switched on in
Settings â†’ Apps. Notifications from every other app are discarded immediately and
never written to storage.

For the apps you enable, Kepta stores:

- Message text
- Sender name, as shown in the notification
- Conversation name
- The date and time the message was sent
- The name of the app that sent it
- Preview images, when an app attaches one to its notification
- Whether the sender later deleted the message

## What Kepta never does

- **No internet.** The app has no network permission and makes no network requests.
- **No account or sign-in.**
- **No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.**
- **No advertising** and no advertising identifiers.
- **No data sharing or selling.** There is no third party to share with.
- **No access to your accounts.** Kepta cannot read your messaging apps' data,
  only the notifications Android shows on screen.

## Where your data is stored

Everything is written to Kepta's private storage area on your device, which
Android isolates from other apps. Preview images are saved as files in that same
private area.

## How long it is kept

You control this in **Settings â†’ Storage and retention**. By default, message text
is kept for one year and photos for 30 days. Anything older is deleted
automatically. You can change both, delete all photos, delete a single
conversation, or erase everything at any time.

Uninstalling Kepta permanently deletes everything it stored.

## Protecting your archive

Kepta can require your fingerprint or screen lock before opening
(**Settings â†’ App lock**), and can blank its preview in the Recents screen. Both are
off by default and are yours to enable.

## Other people's messages

Kepta saves notifications sent to **you**. Those notifications often contain
messages written by other people, who have not agreed to being archived. Please
use it responsibly, respect the privacy of the people who message you, and follow
the laws in your country about recording communications.

Kepta is not designed to monitor another person, and must not be used that way.

## Children

Kepta is not directed at children under 13.
